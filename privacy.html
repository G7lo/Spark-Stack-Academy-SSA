SPARK STACK ACADEMY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Spark Stack Academy and Spark Stack International handle information when you visit, create an account, learn, teach, communicate, pay, receive notifications, use wallets, submit reports, or otherwise interact with the platform. We aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary to operate the service, protect accounts, provide education, process payments and improve reliability.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the feature, information may include name, username, email address, role, profile photo, account identifiers, learning progress, enrollments, course activity, assessment records, certificate records, messages, support requests, payment references, transaction status, device/browser information, approximate technical location, security events, timestamps and preferences. We do not need your payment-card secret credentials to operate ordinary academy services; payment processors handle sensitive payment authentication.

3. Public versus private information

Usernames, display names, role badges and selected profile information may be visible to other authenticated users where needed for collaboration. Email addresses and internal identifiers are not intended to be displayed in the messaging interface. Administrative records, payment information, moderation records and security data are restricted according to role and system permissions.

4. Authentication

Account authentication may use Firebase Authentication, including email/password or supported identity providers. Authentication identifiers allow the platform to associate a signed-in account with its academy profile. We do not ask users to send passwords or one-time authentication codes through messaging or support.

5. Supabase platform data

Operational application data may be stored in Supabase, including profiles, conversations, messages, notifications, payments, reports and other structured records. Access is controlled using application roles, database policies and backend functions where applicable. Realtime messaging may use Supabase Realtime channels for delivery, presence and typing indicators.

6. Messaging privacy

Messages are processed to deliver communications between permitted participants. Realtime typing indicators and online presence are transient application signals and should not be treated as permanent messages. Message metadata such as sender, conversation, creation time and read state may be retained to provide the messaging service, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and maintain security.

7. Payments

Paid transactions may be processed through Pesapal. We may retain payment references, amounts, currency, course identifiers, payment status, provider transaction identifiers, payment method labels and timestamps. We use this information to reconcile payments, grant access, issue receipts or records, detect fraud, handle refunds and answer support questions. We do not intentionally store payment authentication secrets such as card PINs.

8. Learning and academic data

We may process enrollment, course progress, lesson completion, assessments, certificates, achievements and related academic records. These records allow instructors and authorized administrators to deliver learning services, verify achievement, investigate academic integrity issues and provide accurate certificates.

9. Notifications and realtime services

The platform may use realtime subscriptions or event-driven services for messages, notifications, presence and other time-sensitive features. We aim to avoid unnecessary persistent listeners and database writes. Some notifications may be generated from payment, enrollment, academic or administrative events.

10. Cookies and local storage

The platform may use browser storage, session state and similar technologies to remember authentication, UI preferences, temporary drafts, onboarding progress and performance settings. These mechanisms are used to provide the requested service and are not intended to expose private account information.

11. How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate accounts; deliver courses; personalize dashboards; maintain enrollment records; communicate with permitted users; process payments; provide certificates; operate wallets where available; send relevant notifications; prevent fraud and abuse; debug technical failures; improve performance; maintain audit records; comply with legal obligations; and respond to support requests.

12. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate authentication, databases, payments, hosting, analytics, communications or security systems, subject to appropriate contractual or technical controls where applicable. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, safety obligations, fraud investigations or protection of the academy and its users.

13. What we do not sell

We do not intend to sell personal information as a commercial data-broker product. We also do not intentionally expose user email addresses in the platform's username-based messaging experience. Any sharing must serve an operational, legal, security or user-requested purpose consistent with this Policy.

14. Security

We use authentication controls, database access policies, role restrictions, secure transport, backend validation and audit mechanisms appropriate to the platform. No internet service can promise absolute security. Users should protect their devices, avoid sharing credentials, use strong passwords and report suspicious account activity quickly.

15. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including account operation, learning records, payment reconciliation, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by data type. Deleted content may remain temporarily in backups, logs or legally required records.

16. Children's privacy

The academy should not be used by anyone who is not legally eligible for the relevant service. Where a service has an age requirement, users must satisfy it. If we learn that information was collected from a person who should not have used a service, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

17. User rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or clarification regarding your personal information. Some records may need to be retained for security, accounting, legal or dispute purposes. Requests should be made through official academy support channels and may require reasonable identity verification.

18. International processing

Cloud providers and service partners may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate legal, contractual or technical safeguards should apply to such processing.

19. Changes

This Policy may change as the platform, services, providers or laws change. The effective date and version at the top identify the current published version. Material changes may be communicated through reasonable platform notices.

20. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, data requests, security concerns or complaints, use the official Spark Stack Academy support/contact channel. Never include passwords, authentication codes, payment PINs or secret keys in a privacy request.
