SPARK STACK ACADEMY

Administrator onboarding guide

Operations, users, academics, moderation, payments, reports and secure administration.

1. Admin responsibility

Administrative access exists to operate the academy, not to browse private data casually. Use the minimum information needed for the task and document important decisions.

2. Command and dashboard tools

Use dashboard KPIs, student records, instructor management, enrollments, payments, announcements, reports and audit tools according to your assigned permissions. Founder-only controls must remain restricted.

3. User management

Verify role and account status before changing access. Never promote users solely because they request it. Keep role changes accountable and investigate unusual activity.

4. Messaging

Admins may communicate with founders, instructors and students where permitted. Keep messages operational and professional. Do not disclose private email addresses when a username is sufficient.

5. Payments

Use Supabase payment records and Pesapal transaction references for reconciliation. Never request payment PINs or credentials. Investigate duplicate, failed, reversed or suspicious transactions before making manual changes.

6. Reports and moderation

Review reports objectively, preserve evidence, avoid retaliation, and escalate severe safety or security issues. Do not expose reporter identities unnecessarily.

7. Announcements

Publish clear, accurate announcements with appropriate audiences. Avoid unnecessary notifications. Correct mistakes promptly and distinguish operational notices from marketing.

8. Security

Use secure devices and strong authentication. Never share admin sessions. Do not bypass database policies or manipulate records directly unless the approved administrative workflow requires it.

9. Audit discipline

Important administrative actions should have an accountable reason. Avoid bulk edits when a targeted change is enough. Review audit records when investigating incidents.

10. Escalation

Escalate legal, payment fraud, serious security incidents, major data exposure, role disputes and founder-level decisions through the appropriate leadership channel.
