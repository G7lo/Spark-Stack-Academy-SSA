SPARK STACK ACADEMY

Instructor onboarding guide

A practical guide to teaching, learners, reports, messaging and instructor operations.

1. Account setup

Complete your professional profile, username, biography and expertise. Keep profile information accurate. Instructor access is role-controlled; do not attempt to access administrative or founder-only functions.

2. Dashboard

Use the instructor dashboard to monitor assigned courses, learners, progress, reports, announcements and operational tasks. Review the dashboard at the start of each teaching session.

3. Course delivery

Follow the approved curriculum and teaching standards. Keep lessons clear, current and respectful. Record meaningful academic activity through the platform rather than private spreadsheets where the system provides the required workflow.

4. Students

Use student records only for legitimate teaching purposes. Avoid requesting private credentials or unnecessary personal information. Treat academic records as confidential and communicate through approved channels.

5. Messaging

Instructors can message students and permitted administrators. Use usernames, not exposed emails, when identifying users. Online presence and typing indicators are convenience features; do not use them for surveillance or harassment.

6. Reports

Submit reports when you identify academic, safety, technical or conduct issues. Provide factual details, relevant identifiers and evidence without unnecessary personal data. Do not retaliate against a learner for making a legitimate complaint.

7. Assessments and integrity

Apply published assessment criteria consistently. Do not falsify grades, completion records or certificates. Suspected cheating should be documented and escalated through the appropriate workflow.

8. Communication standards

Keep communication professional. Do not pressure students into off-platform payments, personal relationships, confidential arrangements or inappropriate content. Escalate difficult situations instead of handling them secretly.

9. Security

Never share credentials, bypass role controls, export private student lists without authorization, or install unapproved scripts. Report suspicious behavior to academy administration.

10. Daily checklist

Review assigned work, check learner progress, respond to important messages, process reports, keep course material current, verify assessment records and sign out from shared devices.
