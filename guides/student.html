SPARK STACK ACADEMY

Student onboarding guide

Your complete guide to learning, communication, payments, progress and account safety.

Start here

Complete your profile, choose a secure password, confirm your contact information, select a public username, and review your dashboard. Your username is the preferred identity for messaging. Never publish passwords, authentication codes or payment secrets.

Dashboard

The dashboard is your home base. Use the overview to see learning progress, active courses, streaks, achievements, certificates, announcements and important actions. Cards and navigation may change as the academy adds features.

Courses and learning

Open Courses to browse available programs. Course pages provide descriptions, learning outcomes, lessons and progress. Use the course player to continue where you stopped. Complete lessons honestly, review required material, submit assignments where requested, and check progress regularly.

Assessments and certificates

Follow each course's assessment rules. Do not share answers or attempt to manipulate progress. Certificates are issued only when requirements are met and may be reviewed for academic integrity. Keep your certificate verification information private when it contains personal data.

Achievements and XP

Eligible learning actions may award XP, streak progress and badges. Do not attempt to farm rewards through duplicate accounts, automated activity or artificial actions. Reward systems can change and suspicious activity may be reviewed.

Messaging

Students can message permitted instructors. Search by username or display name rather than email. In chat you can see permitted online/last-seen information, typing indicators and read state. Be respectful, avoid sensitive data, and report abuse or suspicious requests.

Payments

Paid enrollments use the displayed Pesapal checkout flow. Confirm the course and amount before paying. Keep your transaction reference. If a payment succeeds but access does not appear, do not pay again immediately; contact support with the reference so the transaction can be reconciled.

Notifications

Notifications may cover enrollment, payment, academic activity, announcements and account events. Read important notices and use the relevant dashboard action rather than responding to suspicious links.

Account and security

Use a unique password, sign out on shared devices, keep your device updated, and never share one-time codes. If you believe your account is compromised, stop using sensitive features and contact support.

Getting help

Use the platform support/help tools when available. Include the page, approximate time, visible error message and transaction or course reference when relevant. Never send your password or payment PIN.
